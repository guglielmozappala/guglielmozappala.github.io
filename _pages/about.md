<div class="about-text" markdown="1">

I am an Assistant Professor at Bocconi University.

My research studies the socio-economic impacts of climate change and humanity’s ability to adapt to these impacts. Part of my research also examines the determinants of environmental preferences and of public support for climate policies.

I obtained my PhD in Economics at the Paris School of Economics. My dissertation was awarded the Best Doctoral Dissertation Award from the [European Association of Environmental and Resource Economists](https://www.eaere.org/best-european-doctoral-dissertation-award) and from the [French Economic Association (AFSE)](https://www.afse.fr/fr/news/laureats-du-prix-de-these-de-l-afse-2024-2356?langue_selected=fr).

Prior to joining Bocconi, I held postdoctoral fellowships at Harvard University, UC Berkeley, and UC Santa Barbara.
