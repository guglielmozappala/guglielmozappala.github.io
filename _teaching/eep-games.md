Three times in the course you stop listening and start trading. Each game puts you inside the market the lecture is about, so that the result is something you found rather than something you were told. The games run on your phone; you join with a nickname, no Bocconi login, and nothing is graded.

| Game | When | The question |
|---|---|---|
| [Game 1: Coase bargaining](/teaching/eep/games/coase/) | Week 5, 9 October | Do property rights and transaction costs matter for reaching an efficient outcome without a regulator? |
| [Game 2: Permit trading](/teaching/eep/games/permits/) | Week 7, 30 October (the whole session) | How does a permit market find its price, and does the way permits are handed out change what happens? |
| [Game 3: Hedonic sorting](/teaching/eep/games/hedonics/) | Week 9, 13 November | How do household preferences and housing supply produce sorting and a price gradient? |

## How to join

All three games live at **[envecon-games.netlify.app](https://envecon-games.netlify.app/)**. Open it on your phone when I tell you to, pick the game, and join with your first name and initial, or with your team's name when the game is played in teams. The QR code is on the first slide of the session as well.

![QR code for the games site](/images/eep/qr-games-home.png){: width="180"}

- One phone per player in the Coase game, one phone per team in the other two.
- Keep the page open during the game: phases, clocks and results update on their own.
- If your phone dies or the page closes, open the link again and join with the same name; you get your seat back.
- Bring a charged phone to the three sessions.

## What happens to the data

Each game leaves a record: which pairs struck a deal and at what price, every permit trade with its timestamp, where each household type chose to live. That record is the material of the following lecture (for the permit game, of the whole of Week 8), and it becomes course material on Blackboard. Names in the record are the nicknames you join with. Nothing you do in a game affects your grade.
