Textbooks, and the podcasts, videos and blog pieces referred to in the [schedule](/teaching/eep/schedule/). Research articles and policy documents are in the reading pack on Blackboard.

## Textbooks

- Charles Kolstad, *Environmental Economics*, Oxford University Press, 2nd edition (2010). The main textbook, cited as K.
- Daniel J. Phaneuf and Till Requate, *A Course in Environmental Economics: Theory, Policy, and Practice*, Cambridge University Press (2016).
- Nathaniel O. Keohane and Sheila M. Olmstead, *Markets and the Environment*, Princeton University Press, 2nd edition (2016).

## Podcasts

- ["Should the Lawns in Vegas Stay in Vegas"](https://www.npr.org/2021/09/02/1033748480/should-the-lawns-in-vegas-stay-in-vegas), *The Indicator from Planet Money*, NPR. Water scarcity and lawn-removal policy in Las Vegas: an everyday example of unpriced resource use and the policy responses to it (Week 2).
- ["A Status Report on Global Emissions Trading"](https://www.resources.org/resources-radio/status-report-global-emissions-trading-stephanie-la-hoz-theuer/), with Stephanie La Hoz Theuer, *Resources Radio*, RFF. The performance of emissions trading systems worldwide, from the EU ETS and RGGI to China's national programme, and the design questions they raise (Week 7).
- ["Pricing Climate Risk in the Markets"](https://www.resources.org/resources-radio/pricing-climate-risk-markets-robert-litterman/), with Robert Litterman, *Resources Radio*, RFF. How financial markets are, and are not, pricing the physical and transition risks of climate change (Week 12).

## Video lectures

- ["Social Cost of Carbon"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNdS1o2GoPQ), Max Auffhammer, UC Berkeley (Week 10).
- ["Planetary Management"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KsHGPSUH7J0), Sol Hsiang, Stanford. How economists estimate the economic damages from climate change and how those estimates feed into the social cost of carbon (Week 10).

## Blog

- ["Discount Rates: A Boring Thing You Should Know About (With Otters!)"](https://grist.org/article/discount-rates-a-boring-thing-you-should-know-about-with-otters/), David Roberts, *Grist*. An accessible introduction to discount rates and to why the Nordhaus and Stern positions on climate action differ so much (Week 10).
